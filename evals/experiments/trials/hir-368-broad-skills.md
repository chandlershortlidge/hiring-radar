10. A broad skill is asked for whenever its name is written in an ask, in any form: as a
    noun, as a modifier of another word ("B platforms", "B libraries", "B infrastructure",
    "B workloads", "B-based", "B-powered"), as its adjective, abbreviated, or in another
    language. Report it even when specific skills stand next to it as its examples — the
    broad skill and each example are separate skills (rules 3 and 4). Before you finish,
    reread each ask for a broad term you have not reported. This adds nothing the text does
    not write: a list of specific skills alone never implies the broad skill they belong to.
